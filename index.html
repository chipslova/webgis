WebGIS Stack

Engineered with modern WebGIS & Earth Observation architectures

  • Core Mapping: MapLibre GL JS v6 (2D Mercator & 3D Globe)
  • Earth Observation: BIG Piksel OGC WMS (Open Data Cube, GeoMAD, Landsat 9)
  • Earth Engine: Google Earth Engine (MODIS LST & Urban Heat Island)
  • Spatial Analytics: Turf.js (Geodesic Distance & Polygon Area)
  • Basemap Services: 15 Vector Styles (Google, Esri, BIG RBI, OSM) & PMTiles
  • Runtime & Bundler: Bun + Vite
